# Where to Knock

You can email me with any proposal at [hire@katokdoes.art](mailto:hire@katokdoes.art). I will definitely read it, and I will definitely reply.

## What I Won’t Do

I won’t do meaningless or pointless work. It doesn’t interest me. I won’t overwork or give my job all of my time. I don’t work with gambling, online casinos, or the porn industry, or with purely commercial projects whose only goal is to make money, plain and simple.

## What I Want to Do (and Will)

It’s much easier for me to work when I understand what the work is for and who needs it. I’m open to collaborating with nonprofits. I’m ready to volunteer on your project if you’re doing something interesting and important for society and don’t have the budget for a good developer. I would be happy if you relocated me to your country.
